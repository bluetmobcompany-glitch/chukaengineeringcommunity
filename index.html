Student registration

Before you submit:

  • Use your official Chuka University email where possible.
  • Your registration number must match your student ID exactly — it's how your class admin finds you.
  • One entry per student. Resubmitting with the same registration number will be rejected.
  • A profile photo link is optional — it's used on personnel/sports pages if you're ever listed there.
  • You get 7 days of free access. After that, a one-time KES 100 activation fee (M-Pesa or card) keeps your account active — see the Activate page.
  • Want to join the Engineering Club too? That's a separate, short sign-up on the Club page.
