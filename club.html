Sheet 05 — Engineering Club

Join the Engineering Club

Open to every year of study. The club runs its own projects, talks and site visits alongside your class activities — sign up once here, no matter what year you're already registered under.

Before you submit:

  • This is a separate sign-up from your class registration on the home page — both are needed if you want to be on the club roster.
  • Your registration number should match your student ID, so club admins can confirm your class year.
  • Pick as many interest areas as apply — this helps the club place you on the right project team.
  • Membership starts as Pending until the club's general admin confirms it.
